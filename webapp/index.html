What Am I Looking At? — Glossary
EMA 20 / 50 / 100 / 200Exponential Moving Averages — the average closing price over the last 20/50/100/200 hourly candles, weighted toward the most recent price. When price sits above all four and they're stacked in order (20 above 50 above 100 above 200), that's a classic strong-uptrend signature; stacked the other way signals a downtrend.
Entry / Stop / TP1–TP3The engine's proposed trade, when it has one active. Entry is where it would get in, Stop is where it would exit if wrong (the most it's risking), and TP1/TP2/TP3 are take-profit targets at 1×, 2× and a final structure-based multiple of that risk.
OTE zone (Optimal Trade Entry)The 62%–79% retracement band of the current price range — a Fibonacci-based zone that ICT/Smart Money traders treat as the highest-probability place to enter in the direction of the bigger trend, rather than chasing price at the extremes.
Dealing range (Range hi / Range lo)The recent swing-high-to-swing-low trading range everything else on this chart is measured against — the OTE zone and the premium/discount split both come from dividing up this range.
Premium / extended zone (red shading)The top portion of the range, above the OTE zone. Price here is statistically "expensive" relative to the recent range — buying up here means chasing the move rather than getting a favorable price.
Discount zone (green shading)The bottom portion of the range, below the OTE zone. Price here is "cheap" relative to the recent range — generally the more favorable area to be a buyer.
Fair Value Gap (FVG)A 3-candle imbalance — price moved so fast between two candles that a gap was left with no trading in between. Price often returns to "fill" this gap before continuing in its original direction, so an unfilled FVG can act like a magnet, and also like support or resistance.
Order Block (OB)The last candle of the opposite colour right before a strong, fast move. The theory is this is where large institutional orders were placed, so if price returns to that same candle's range later, it can act as support or resistance again.
BOS (Break of Structure)Price breaks the last significant swing high or low in the same direction as the existing trend — read as confirmation the trend is continuing.
CHoCH (Change of Character)Price breaks the last significant swing high or low against the existing trend — an early warning sign the trend may be correcting or reversing, not just pausing.
